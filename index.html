Our Story

Two Decades of Lucknow's Sweetness

RajLuxmi Sweets & Restaurant has been a trusted name for sweetness and taste in Alambagh, Lucknow since 2004. Located opposite Chander Nagar Gate, near Alambagh Metro Station, our shop is not just a sweet shop — it is a complete family restaurant.

Here, under one roof, you get fresh sweets and bakery along with the full range of North Indian, Awadhi, South Indian, Chinese and street food — everything 100% pure vegetarian. Whether it is festive sweets, a child's birthday cake, or an evening meal with the family — RajLuxmi is a companion for every occasion.
