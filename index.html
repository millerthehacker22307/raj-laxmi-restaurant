Hamari Kahani

Do Dashaak se Lucknow ki Mithaas

RajLuxmi Sweets & Restaurant 2004 se Alambagh, Lucknow me mithaas aur swaad ka bharosa bana hua hai. Chander Nagar Gate ke saamne, Alambagh Metro Station ke paas sthit hamari dukaan sirf ek mithai ki shop nahi — ek poora family restaurant hai.

Yahan ek hi jagah milti hai taazi mithai aur bakery ke saath North Indian, Awadhi, South Indian, Chinese aur street food ki poori range — sab kuch shudh shakahari. Tyohar ki mithaas ho, bachon ke birthday ka cake, ya parivaar ke saath shaam ka khana — RajLuxmi har mauke ka saathi hai.
